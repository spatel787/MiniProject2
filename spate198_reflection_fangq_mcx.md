# Reflection: fangq_mcx

MCX is different from most of the other projects because its overall pattern is U-shaped. The seven-month gap came after version 0.9.7-2 documentation, bug fixes, and release cleanup. Qianqian Fang then returned with seed replay, GPU querying, CUDA 6.5 compatibility, and multi-GPU support. The gap was easy to locate but hard to explain; the timing looks like a pause between release cycles, although the messages do not prove it. MCX recovered strongly and is active under the assignment cutoff.
