# Reflection: quantumblacklabs_kedro-viz

Kedro-Viz was the simplest case because its longest zero-commit period was only two months. That does not meet the assignment's three-month definition of a long gap. Work returned with the CarbonViz-to-KernelViz rename and then chart, synchronization, packaging, and error-handling changes. Ivan Danov and Richard Westenra were already involved before the pause, so there was no clear contributor turnover. I see this as a short pause during early development, not a project shutdown.
