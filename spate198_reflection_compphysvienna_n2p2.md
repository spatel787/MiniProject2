# Reflection: compphysvienna_n2p2

N2P2's fifteen-month gap was the second-longest one in my data. Before it, the commits covered 4G-HDNNP training, build fixes, and `pynnp`; afterward, they added a production predictor and CMake support. [PR #188](https://github.com/CompPhysVienna/n2p2/pull/188) also brought an updated LAMMPS interface and Cython 3 work. The restart is clear, but the gap itself was hard to interpret because the record does not explain the delay. New contributors joined the restart, but N2P2 is inactive under the assignment's fixed cutoff date.
